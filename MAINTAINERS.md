# Maintainers

## Primary maintainer

- **@alanua** — project owner and primary maintainer.

The primary maintainer is responsible for product scope, architecture direction, issue triage, pull-request review, release/deployment decisions, access-control/security changes, and final merge authority.

## Decision model

AI tools and automated runners may assist with implementation, tests, review, and maintenance workflows, but they are not maintainers and do not hold independent merge, deployment, or data-access authority.

## Contribution path

Contributors should follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
