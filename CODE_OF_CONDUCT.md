# Code of Conduct

BauClock welcomes technical collaboration conducted professionally and respectfully.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss code, product behavior, and evidence without harassment or personal attacks;
- respect privacy and the sensitivity of employment/time data;
- avoid publishing secrets, personal records, or exploit details;
- keep issues and pull requests focused on the project;
- follow maintainer requests intended to protect security, data integrity, or legal/compliance boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, or attempts to bypass project security controls.

Maintainers may moderate, edit, lock, or remove discussions/contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project.

Sensitive conduct or security concerns should be raised privately with the primary maintainer where possible.
